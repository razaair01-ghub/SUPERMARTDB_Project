Delivery Performance and Customer Experience
Business Question:
What’s the average delivery time, and how does it impact customer satisfaction and repeat orders?
SQL Task:
•	Use Orders table with OrderDate and ShipDate.
•	Compute delivery time = DATEDIFF(ShipDate, OrderDate).
•	Join with Customer Feedback or Repeat Orders (if available).
•	Segment customers based on delivery performance.
Power BI Visualization:
•	Histogram of delivery times
•	KPI: Avg Delivery Time
•	Line chart: Delivery time vs Repeat Purchase Rate
•	Slicer for shipping mode
